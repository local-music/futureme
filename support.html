Frequently asked questions

When can I open a sealed capsule?

A sealed capsule becomes available when its chosen unlock date arrives.

Can I edit a draft?

Yes. You can return to a saved draft and finish it before sealing.

How do I restore Premium?

Open the Premium screen in the app and use the Restore Purchases option. Ensure you're signed in with the Apple Account used for the original purchase.

My countdown or widget isn't updating.

Open FUTUREME to refresh its data, then check the widget again. If the problem persists, restart your device and contact support.

How can I request a refund?

Apple manages App Store purchases and refunds. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request one.
