Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 October 2026

1. About FUTUREME

FUTUREME is a personal journaling and time capsule app provided by the FUTUREME developer. Privacy questions can be sent to support@kook.love.

2. Your letters and memories

Letters, time capsules, journal entries, photos, and audio recordings you add to FUTUREME are stored locally on your device. FUTUREME does not upload this personal content to a FUTUREME-operated server.

Depending on your device's settings, local app data may be included in backups managed by Apple. Backup and restoration are governed by your device and Apple account settings.

3. Device permissions

FUTUREME may ask for access to photos or the microphone when you choose to attach media, and for notification permission when you enable reminders. Permissions can be changed in iOS Settings.

4. Premium purchases

Optional Premium subscriptions and lifetime purchases are processed through Apple's App Store. FUTUREME does not directly collect your payment card details. Apple may process purchase and transaction information under its own privacy policy. The app uses purchase entitlement information to determine access to Premium features.

5. Analytics and external services

FUTUREME does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or third-party crash-reporting services. The app is built using Apple technologies including SwiftUI, local device storage, and StoreKit. Apple may separately collect diagnostics and App Store information according to your device settings and Apple’s privacy practices.

6. Sharing

FUTUREME does not send your locally stored letters, journal entries, photos, or audio recordings to a FUTUREME-operated server. Premium purchase transactions are handled by Apple through the App Store.

7. Retention and deletion

Your content remains on your device until you delete it within the app or remove the app, subject to any device backups. Removing the app generally deletes its local app container; device backups may retain copies until removed according to your backup settings.

8. Security

Your content is stored locally and benefits from applicable iOS device security protections. Protect your device with a passcode and keep iOS updated.

9. Children's privacy

FUTUREME is a general-purpose personal journaling app and does not knowingly collect personal journal content on developer-operated servers. If you have questions about use by a child, contact us at support@kook.love.

10. Updates

This policy may change when FUTUREME's features or data practices change. The updated policy and effective date will appear on this page.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, contact support@kook.love.
